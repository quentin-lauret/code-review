Rubber Duck Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

The short version: Rubber Duck does not collect any personal data.

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • Your progress is stored only on your iPhone and never leaves it.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

1. Who we are

Rubber Duck (the “app”) is developed and published by Quentin Lauret, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the app handles your information. If you have a question, contact us at quentin.lauret@epita.fr.

2. Data we collect

We do not collect, receive or store any personal data. The app has no servers of its own and sends nothing over the network to us or to anyone else. We do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or any other third-party service, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

3. Data stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your iPhone, in the app’s local storage. This data stays on your device. We cannot see it or access it.

WhatWhy
Your answers to the onboarding questions (learning goal, daily goal) and placement test resultsTo build your learning path
Learning progress: lessons completed, answers given, mistakes to review, XP, streaks, Daily resultsTo show your progress and choose your next exercises
Settings: haptics, appearance, streak reminder on/off and reminder timeTo remember your preferences

The app does not currently sync this data with iCloud. If your iPhone’s own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) are turned on, iOS may include this data in them. Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules one local reminder a day at the time you choose in Settings. The reminder is created and delivered on your device. No push notification service is used and no information is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the app’s Settings, or withdraw permission at any time in iOS Settings > Notifications > Rubber Duck. The app works fully with notifications off.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Rubber Duck Pro is sold as an auto-renewable subscription through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never receive your name, email address or payment details. The app checks whether your subscription is active using Apple’s StoreKit framework, directly on your device. We do not run any server for purchases and the app does not save any subscription information of its own. Apple’s handling of your purchase data is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.

6. Sharing

We do not share, sell or rent any data, because we do not have any. If you tap “Share result” after a Daily exercise, the app opens the iOS share sheet with a short summary (the Daily number, your time and a row of colored squares). It contains no personal information. You choose whether and where to send it, and the app or service you pick handles it under its own privacy policy.

Since the app does not share user data with any third party, no third party has access to it. If this ever changes, any third party would have to provide the same or equal protection of user data as this policy, and we would update this policy before the change takes effect.

7. Retention and deletion

Data stored by the app stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time in two ways:

  • In the app, go to Settings and tap “Reset all progress & delete my data”. This permanently erases your progress and settings from your iPhone.
  • Delete the app from your iPhone. This removes all of its data from the device.

Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription. Subscriptions are managed by Apple, as described in section 5.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) give you rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, and to object to its processing. Because we do not collect or hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access or delete on our side. Everything the app stores is on your device and under your control, as described in section 7. If you have a question or a request, email us at quentin.lauret@epita.fr and we will reply within 30 days.

9. Children

Rubber Duck is meant for developers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

10. Contacting support

If you tap “Contact support” in the app, it opens your email app with a message addressed to us. If you send it, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you, and we delete the conversation once your request is resolved, unless the law requires us to keep it longer. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change applies. If a change means the app starts collecting data, we will ask for your consent in the app first.
